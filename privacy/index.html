Privacy Policy

inspo ideas Pin Scheduler, operated by inspo ideas. Effective October 27, 2026.

inspo ideas Pin Scheduler is a private tool that helps inspo ideas write and schedule posts about products for its own Pinterest and Threads accounts. It is used only by its owner. It does not offer accounts to the public and does not collect information from other people.

Who we are

The app is operated by inspo ideas. Questions about this policy can be sent to liftaah@gmail.com.

What data the app accesses

When the owner connects their own accounts, the app accesses only what it needs to publish and measure the owner's posts:

  • Pinterest: an access token for the owner's account, the ID of the owner's board, the Pins the app creates, and performance numbers for those Pins (impressions, outbound clicks and saves).
  • Threads: an access token for the owner's account, the owner's Threads user ID, the posts the app publishes, and performance numbers for those posts (views and likes).
  • Product information that the owner enters manually, such as product names, prices and affiliate links.

The app does not access other users' profiles, followers, messages, or any personal information about other people.

How the data is used

  • To publish Pins and posts that the owner has individually reviewed and approved.
  • To show the owner how their own posts perform, so they can decide what to post next.

Text for posts may be drafted with the help of an AI service (Anthropic's Claude API). Only product information is sent to it. Access tokens and account data are never sent to the AI service.

Data from Pinterest is never sent to the AI service and is never used to train, fine-tune or improve any AI or machine learning model.

Where the data is stored

  • Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private GitHub repository and are never written into code, logs or spreadsheets.
  • Post drafts, schedules and performance numbers for the owner's own Pins and posts are stored in the owner's private Google Sheet. No content or data about other Pinterest users is stored.

The app does not store passwords or login cookies. Sign-in happens only through the official Pinterest and Threads authorization screens.

Sharing

The data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties for advertising or any other purpose. It is processed only by the services named above (Pinterest, Threads, GitHub, Google and Anthropic) to run the app for its owner.

Retention

Access tokens are kept only while the accounts stay connected and are replaced when they are refreshed. Post records and performance numbers are kept in the owner's Google Sheet until the owner deletes them.

Data deletion

To remove the app's access and delete its data:

  • Revoke the app's access in your Pinterest settings (Security and logins → Apps) or in your Threads settings (Account → Website permissions).
  • Delete the stored tokens from the GitHub repository secrets.
  • Delete the related rows or the whole Google Sheet.

You can also request deletion by emailing liftaah@gmail.com. Requests are completed within 30 days.

Security

Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets, connections use HTTPS, and the code repository is private. Access is limited to the minimum permissions needed to publish and read performance of the owner's own posts.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
